## Notes for whoever writes the create script

- Duplicate check: read-only ILIKE search on name, short_name and city for every school (plus state/city terms). The organizations table holds high schools from Missouri only, so none of the 28 out-of-state schools exists. No LINK candidates; the near-misses (Providence Academy HS, College Park Christian Acad Jr, Lutheran rows, Olathe NW) are different schools.
- JSON `org` uses the organizations column names (camelCase, as in the college script). `abbreviation` is the meet code, as the college script did; the MSHSAA seed rows use the short name there instead.
- Logos are in `gans-hs-logos/`, named by school slug; `logo.suggestedStorageKey` proposes a `hs/` folder in the Logos bucket (existing folders: mshsaa, naia, ncaa, generic). Only Sioux City North has a reversed (white) file, for logoDarkUrl. Several are derived: viewBox or raster crops of a school lockup (Ankeny Centennial, Pleasant Valley, SM South, SM West, Sioux City North, Shiloh, Pulaski, Murphysboro, Metro-East Lutheran) and one recolour (Waukee Northwest paw). Bridgeland's file is 1.2 MB; Bentonville, Fayetteville, North Polk and Millard West are small (136-201 px).
- State associations: org_tags has only MSHSAA as a high-school governing body, so IHSAA (Iowa), AAA, UIL, KSHSAA, NSAA, IHSA, TSSAA, OSSAA, CHSAA and GAPPS tags would be new. Iowa's "IHSAA" collides with Indiana's IHSAA — give the tag a state-qualified slug (e.g. ihsaa-ia). Iowa girls compete under IGHSAU; stateAssociation holds the boys' body.
- Two teams sent a single runner (Johnson Ferry Christian Academy, Pulaski Academy) and Sioux City North one entry; they are still real schools.
